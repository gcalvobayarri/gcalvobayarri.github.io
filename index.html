About me

My research focuses on the development and application of Bayesian statistical models, particularly for complex longitudinal and multivariate data. I also teach statistics and bio-statistics at the University of Valencia.

Selected publications

A selection of my first-author journal articles. All are freely available through the links below.

Selecting the Best Compositions of a Wheelchair Basketball Team: A Data-Driven Approach

The American Statistician, 2025 Open access

This study combines a Bayesian longitudinal model with integer optimisation to turn players’ past performance into optimal wheelchair basketball line-ups. It provides a practical bridge between statistical inference and sporting decisions.

Read article →

Can the Hot Hand Phenomenon Be Modelled? A Bayesian Hidden Markov Approach

Computational Statistics, 2025 Open access

A Bayesian hidden Markov model represents basketball shots through latent hot and cold states and quantifies the strength of team streaks probabilistically. Applications to NBA teams move the hot-hand debate beyond simple runs of makes and misses.

Read article →

Bayes Factors for Longitudinal Model Assessment via Power Posteriors

Communications in Statistics – Simulation and Computation, 2026 Open access

This work extends power posteriors to compute Bayes factors for longitudinal models with random effects, where marginal likelihoods are especially demanding. The approach is assessed through simulation studies and Mediterranean sardine data.

Read article →

Bayesian Joint Longitudinal Models for Assessing the Exploitation Rates of Sardine Stock in the Mediterranean Sea

Stochastic Environmental Research and Risk Assessment, 2024 Open access

Joint longitudinal models trace artisanal and industrial sardine catches across Mediterranean countries from 1985 to 2018. The analysis uncovers an overall decline while revealing important differences between countries and fishing sectors.

Read article →

Bayesian Hierarchical Nonlinear Modelling of Intra-abdominal Volume During Pneumoperitoneum for Laparoscopic Surgery

SORT, 2021 Open access

Bayesian hierarchical nonlinear models describe how intra-abdominal volume changes with insufflation pressure while accounting for patient variability. The study helps identify a balance between surgical working space and patient safety.
