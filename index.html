Gabriel Calvo Bayarri

I work on Bayesian hierarchical and longitudinal models — building methodology for repeated-measures and time-dependent data, and hidden Markov structures — with applications spanning sports analytics, fisheries science and biomedicine. I completed my PhD jointly at the Universitat de València and BioSS, and now teach statistics and biostatistics at the Universitat de València.

About

My PhD thesis, Bayesian hierarchical modelling for repeated measures and longitudinal data, was supervised by Carmen Armero (Universitat de València) and Luigi Spezia (Biomathematics & Statistics Scotland), and combined stays in Aberdeen and Valencia. It developed hierarchical and hidden Markov approaches for data that unfolds over time — work that now spans wheelchair basketball line-ups, NBA shot sequences, Mediterranean sardine stocks and surgical monitoring. I currently teach statistics and biostatistics at the Universitat de València and remain an associate of BioSS.

Selecting the Best Compositions of a Wheelchair Basketball Team: A Data-Driven Approach

The American Statistician · 2025 Open access

Combines a Bayesian longitudinal model with integer optimisation to turn players' past performance into optimal wheelchair basketball line-ups, bridging statistical inference and sporting decisions.

Can the Hot Hand Phenomenon Be Modelled? A Bayesian Hidden Markov Approach

Computational Statistics · 2025 Open access

A Bayesian hidden Markov model represents basketball shots through latent hot and cold states, quantifying the strength of team streaks probabilistically and moving the hot-hand debate beyond simple runs of makes and misses.

Read article →

Bayes Factors for Longitudinal Model Assessment via Power Posteriors

Communications in Statistics – Simulation and Computation · 2026 Open access

Extends power posteriors to compute Bayes factors for longitudinal models with random effects, where marginal likelihoods are especially demanding, assessed through simulation studies and Mediterranean sardine data.

Read article →

Bayesian Joint Longitudinal Models for Assessing the Exploitation Rates of Sardine Stock in the Mediterranean Sea

Stochastic Environmental Research and Risk Assessment · 2024 Open access

Joint longitudinal models trace artisanal and industrial sardine catches across Mediterranean countries from 1985 to 2018, uncovering an overall decline alongside important differences between countries and fishing sectors.

Bayesian Hierarchical Nonlinear Modelling of Intra-abdominal Volume During Pneumoperitoneum for Laparoscopic Surgery

SORT · 2021 Open access

Bayesian hierarchical nonlinear models describe how intra-abdominal volume changes with insufflation pressure while accounting for patient variability, helping identify a balance between surgical working space and patient safety.
